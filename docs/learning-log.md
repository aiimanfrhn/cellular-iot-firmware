# Learning Log

One dated entry per week of the [interview prep plan](interview-prep-plan.md), and later the [firmware project plan](firmware-project-plan.md). Keep entries short and specific: the point is to see what is improving and what keeps coming back.

Most numbers come straight from the trainer. The progress card shows problems solved, each problem shows your best timed solve, and the mock interview test keeps your scores.

## Entry template

### YYYY-MM-DD · Week N: topic

- **Goal:** what this week was meant to cover
- **Studied:** guides read and quiz scores
- **Solved:** problems passed, with times against target; mark any that needed hints or the solution
- **Mock test:** score, and the topics it flagged (if you took one)
- **Hard this week:** the concept or bug that took longest, and how you got past it
- **Explain out loud:** one follow-up question you can now answer well, and one you can't yet
- **Job search:** applications, replies and interviews, and anything an interviewer asked that you want to practise
- **Next week:** the one thing to focus on
